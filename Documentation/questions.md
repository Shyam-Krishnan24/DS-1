
## 1. Proposed project
**Project title:** Olist E-Commerce Business Intelligence & Predictive Analytics

**Business problem statement:**
    How can an e-commerce business use historical order, customer, product, payment, seller, delivery and review data to understand
    its sales performance, identify factors associated with customer satisfaction and delivery delays, and predict future business outcomes to improve operational decisions?

## 2. Historical Questions:

1. How fast is the business growing, and is its growth continuing or flattening?
2. Which product categories generate the most revenue, and how concentrated is revenue across categories?
3. Which states or regions drive sales, and how do their delivery times differ?
4. How do sales and order volumes change over time, including seasonal and peak-demand periods?
5. What proportion of orders are cancelled or unavailable?
6. How often are orders delivered late, and where are late deliveries concentrated?
7. How strongly are late deliveries associated with lower review scores?
8. Are estimated delivery dates realistic compared with actual delivery times?
9. Does the distance between sellers and customers relate to delivery time and lateness?
10. Which payment methods and installment patterns do customers use, and are they associated with customer satisfaction?
11. Do a small number of sellers account for a disproportionate share of late deliveries and poor reviews?
12. What proportion of customers place repeat orders, and is a good first experience associated with repeat purchasing?
13. When do customers place orders by day of the week and hour?
14. How did peak demand, particularly Black Friday in November 2017, affect order volumes and delivery performance?
15. Which product categories have the highest average order value?
16. Do customers with more purchases spend more overall?
17. Are there relationships between order value, freight cost, delivery time and review score?

## 3. Predictive Questions:

**A. Classification**
1. Can we predict at purchase time whether an order will arrive late?
2. Can we predict which orders are likely to receive a low review score (1–2 stars)?

**B. Regression**
3. Can we predict how many days an order will take to be delivered?
4. Can we estimate more accurate delivery promises using predicted delivery times?

**C. Forecasting**
5. How many orders should we expect each month over the next six months?
6. How much revenue should we expect each month over the next six months?
7. How much additional order volume and revenue might Black Friday and December peak demand generate?
8. What late-delivery rate should we expect during peak months, and how many low-review orders might result?
9. Which product categories are likely to gain or lose sales share, helping businesses plan inventory?
