OLIST E-COMMERCE DATA SCIENCE PROJECT
====================================

Current Progress
----------------
- Inspected eight Olist CSV datasets and reviewed their columns and data types.
- Checked missing values and duplicate rows.
- Validated order, item, product, seller, customer, payment, and review references.
- Checked the order-item composite key and geolocation fields.
- Converted order timestamps to datetime for future analysis.

Key Findings
------------
- Missing review titles (88.34%) and messages (58.70%) are retained; review scores remain available for analysis.
- Some order delivery timestamps are missing. These will be considered only where relevant to each analysis.
- 160 orders (0.16%) have no approval timestamp: 141 cancelled, 5 created, and 14 delivered.
- Delivery duration for the 14 delivered orders with missing approval timestamps ranges from 10 to 18 days (mean: 12.71; median: 12). This does not explain the missing approval times. Retain these orders and timestamps as recorded; do not invent dates.
- Product category is missing for 0.01% of products.
- No unmatched order, item, product, seller, customer, payment, or review references were found in the checks performed.
- No missing ZIP prefixes or coordinates were found. Multiple geolocation records may share a ZIP prefix, so do not remove them solely for that reason.
